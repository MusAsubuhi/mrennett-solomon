Think about what you do in the room before you think about the scent. A hallway is a place you pass through. A bedroom is a place you stay. The scent can follow that difference.

## Start with the pace of the room

Rooms you pass through often suit a light, green scent. Rooms where you slow down suit something softer and more floral.

## Place it where you will notice it

A shelf at eye level or a side table works well. Keep it away from direct sun, and give it a little space around it.

## Let it settle

Scent takes a moment to fill a room. Check back in an hour before you decide whether you like it.
