An evening routine does not need to be long. A few repeated steps can tell you the day is ending.

## 1. Lower the lights

Switch off the bright lights and use a lamp instead. A softer room sets the pace.

## 2. Take your time in the shower

Give yourself a few extra minutes. Put your phone in another room and let the water run.

## 3. Set the room

Put out fresh towels, open a window for a moment and let the room settle before bed.
