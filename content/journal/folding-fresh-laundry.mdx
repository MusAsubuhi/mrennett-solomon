Most of us rush through laundry. It does not have to be that way. Folding can be a small pause in a busy week.

## Fold while it is still fresh

Take towels and sheets out of the line or the dryer as soon as you can. Shake them out and fold them while they are still warm or just dry.

## Make it a ritual

Put on some music, open a window and fold one thing at a time. Stack the folded linen where you will see it.

## Finish with the bed

Making the bed with fresh sheets is a good place to end. It is a small reward for the work.
