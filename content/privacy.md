## What this page covers

This page explains, in plain language, what information this website handles and why. It is a draft and needs review by the owner and a legal adviser before launch.

## Information you give us

- **Orders.** When you choose Order on WhatsApp, your order is sent as a WhatsApp message. We see the details you put in that message, such as your name, delivery area and payment method. WhatsApp has its own privacy terms.
- **Emails.** If you sign up for emails, we ask for your email address and your consent. We use it only to send you Solo's news.
- **Messages.** If you use the contact form, we use your name, email address and message to reply to you.

## Consent and your choices

We ask for your clear consent before sending marketing emails, and you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us. Where data protection law applies to us, including Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you.

## Your cart

Your cart is saved in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you place an order.

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If analytics are switched on, they are limited to simple, privacy-respecting counts such as page views and order-button clicks.

## Contact

To ask a question about your data or to withdraw consent, please use the contact page.
